Rowwad Translation Center — 399

29:24 His people’s only response was to say, “Kill him or burn him!” But Allah saved him from the fire[1]. Indeed, there are signs in this for people who believe.

29:25 He said, “You have taken idols other than Allah as a means of love among yourselves in the life of this world, but on the Day of Resurrection you will disown and curse one another. Your abode will be the Fire, and you will have no helpers.”

29:26 Lot believed in him, and Abraham said, “I am emigrating to my Lord. He is indeed the All-Mighty, the All-Wise.”

29:27 We gave him Isaac and Jacob, and assigned prophethood and scripture to his offspring, and gave him his reward in this life[1], and in the Hereafter he will surely be among the righteous.

29:28 And [remember] Lot, when he said to his people, “You surely commit a shameful act that no one in the world has ever done before you.

29:29 Do you approach men with lust, engage in highway robbery[1], and practice immoral acts in your gatherings?” But the only response his people gave was to say, “Bring us Allah’s punishment, if you are truthful.”

29:30 He said, “My Lord, help me against people who spread corruption.”
