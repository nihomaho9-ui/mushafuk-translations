Rowwad Translation Center — 215

10:54 If every wrongdoer were to possess all that is on earth, he would surely offer it to ransom himself. They will hide their remorse[1] when they see the punishment. They will be judged in all fairness, and they will not be wronged.

10:55 Indeed, to Allah belongs all that is in the heavens and earth. Allah’s promise is certainly true, but most of them do not know.

10:56 It is He Who gives life and causes death, and to Him you will all be returned.

10:57 O people, there has come to you an exhortation from your Lord, a cure for [illness] of the hearts, a guidance and mercy for the believers.

10:58 Say, “By Allah’s grace and His mercy let them therefore rejoice. It is much better than what they accumulate[1].”

10:59 Say [to the pagans], “What do you think about the provision that Allah has sent down to you, of which you made some unlawful and some lawful[1].” Say, “Has Allah given you permission or are you fabricating lies against Allah?”

10:60 What do those who fabricate lies against Allah think of it on the Day of Judgment[1]? Indeed, Allah is bountiful to people, yet most of them are ungrateful.

10:61 Whatever matter you [O Prophet] may be engaged in, and whatever portion of the Qur’an you may recite, and whatever deed you [O people] may do, We are a Witness over you when you are engaged in it. Nothing is hidden from your Lord, not even an atom’s weight on earth nor in heaven, nor anything smaller or greater than that, except that it is [written] in a clear Record[1].
