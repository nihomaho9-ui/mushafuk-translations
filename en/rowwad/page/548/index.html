Rowwad Translation Center — 548

59:17 So they will both end up in the Fire, abiding therein forever. That is the recompense of the wrongdoers.

59:18 O you who believe, fear Allah, and let every soul consider what it has sent forth for tomorrow[1]. And fear Allah. Indeed, Allah is All-Aware of what you do.

59:19 Do not be like those who forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves. It is they who are the evildoers.

59:20 The people of Hell and the people of Paradise are not equal. It is the people of Paradise who will triumph.

59:21 Had We sent down this Qur’an upon a mountain, you would have seen it humbled and break asunder out of awe of Allah[1]. Such are the similitudes We set forth for people so that they may reflect.

59:22 He is Allah; none has the right to be worshiped except Him, Knower of the unseen and the seen; He is the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful.

59:23 He is Allah; none has the right to be worshiped except Him, the Sovereign[1], the Most Holy[2], the Most Perfect[3], the Granter of Security[4], the Watcher over all, the All-Mighty, the Compeller[5], the Supreme[6]. Glory be to Allah far above what they associate with Him.

59:24 He is Allah, the Creator[1], the Maker[2], the Fashioner[3]. He has the most beautiful names. All that is in the heavens and earth glorifies Him, for He is the All-Mighty, the All-Wise.
