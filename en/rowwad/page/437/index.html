Rowwad Translation Center — 437

35:19 The blind and the seeing are not equal[1],

35:20 nor are the depths of darkness and the light[1],

35:21 nor the shade and the scorching wind[1].

35:22 Nor are the living and the dead equal. Allah makes to hear whom He wills, but you cannot make to hear those who are in the graves[1].

35:23 You are but a warner.

35:24 We have sent you with the truth as a bearer of glad tidings and a warner. There has never been any community except that a warner came to it[1].

35:25 If they reject you, so did those who came before them. Their messengers came to them with clear proofs, divine scriptures[1], and an enlightening Book[2].

35:26 Then I seized those who disbelieved. How severe was My response!

35:27 Do you not see that Allah sends down rain from the sky, then We bring forth thereby fruits of various colors? And in the mountains there are various tracts of white and red, of various colors, and raven black;

35:28 just as humans, animals and livestock are of various colors. Only the people of knowledge among His slaves fear Allah. Indeed, Allah is All-Mighty, All-Forgiving.

35:29 Those who recite the Book of Allah, establish prayer, and spend out of what We have provided for them, secretly and openly, hoping for a deal that will suffer no loss,

35:30 so that He will reward them in full and give them more out of His bounty[1]. He is All-Forgiving, Most Appreciative.
