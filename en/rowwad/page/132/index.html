Rowwad Translation Center — 132

6:36 Only those who listen will respond. As for the dead[1], Allah will resurrect them, then to Him they will be returned.

6:37 They say, “Why has no sign been sent down to him from his Lord?” Say, “Allah is Capable to send down a sign,” but most of them do not know [the consequences].

6:38 There is no moving creature on earth or a bird flying with its two wings, but are communities like you. We have missed nothing in the Record[1], then to their Lord they will be gathered.

6:39 Those who reject Our verses are deaf and dumb in the depths of darkness. Whomever Allah wills, He causes him to stray and whomever He wills, He leads him to a straight path.

6:40 Say, “What do you think[1], if there comes upon you the punishment of Allah or the Hour comes upon you, would you then call upon anyone other than Allah, if you are truthful?”

6:41 No, it is Him alone you would call upon[1]. If He willed, He could remove whatever harm made you call upon Him, and you would forget whatever partners you associate with Him.”

6:42 We sent messengers to nations before you and seized them with poverty and hardship, so that they may humble themselves.

6:43 If only they had humbled themselves when Our affliction came upon them, but their hearts were hardened, and Satan made their misdeeds appealing to them.

6:44 When they forgot the reminder that they were given[1], We opened for them the doors of everything – until when they rejoiced in pride for what they were given, We seized them by surprise, so they fell into utter despair.
