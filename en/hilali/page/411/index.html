Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 411

31:1 1. Alif-Lâm-Mîm.

31:2 2. These are Verses of the Wise Book (the Qur’ân).

31:3 3. A guide and a mercy for the Muhsinûn (good-doers).[1]

31:4 4. Those who perform As-Salât (Iqamat-as-Salât) and give Zakât and they have faith in the Hereafter with certainty.

31:5 5. Such are on guidance from their Lord, and such are the successful.

31:6 6. And of mankind is he who purchases idle talks (i.e. music, singing, etc.) to mislead (men) from the Path of Allâh without knowledge, and takes it (the Path of Allâh, or the Verses of the Qur’ân) by way of mockery. For such there will be a humiliating torment (in the Hell-fire).[2]

31:7 7. And when Our Verses (of the Qur’ân) are recited to such a one, he turns away in pride,[3] as if he heard them not - as if there were deafness in his ear. So announce to him a painful torment.

31:8 8. Verily, those who believe (in Islâmic Monotheism) and do righteous good deeds, for them are Gardens of Delight (Paradise).

31:9 9. To abide therein. It is a Promise of Allâh in truth. And He is the All-Mighty, the All-Wise.

31:10 10. He has created the heavens without any pillars that you see, and has set on the earth firm mountains lest it should shake with you. And He has scattered therein moving (living) creatures of all kinds. And We send down water (rain) from the sky, and We cause (plants) of every goodly kind to grow therein.

31:11 11. This is the creation of Allâh. So show Me that which those (whom you worship) besides Him have created. Nay, the Zâlimûn (polytheists, wrongdoers and those who do not believe in the Oneness of Allâh) are in plain error.
