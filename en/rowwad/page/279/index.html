Rowwad Translation Center — 279

16:103 We surely know that they say, “It is only a human being who teaches him[1].” But the language they refer to is foreign, whereas this is eloquent Arabic language.

16:104 Those who do not believe in the verses of Allah, Allah will not guide them, and for them there will be a painful punishment.

16:105 No one fabricates lies except those who do not believe in the verses of Allah; it is they who are the liars.

16:106 Whoever disbelieves in Allah after having believed – except one who is compelled while his heart is firm in faith [has incurred Allah’s wrath] – but those who embrace disbelief willingly, upon them will be the wrath of Allah, and for them there will be a great punishment.

16:107 That is because they prefer the life of this world to the Hereafter, and Allah does not guide the disbelieving people.

16:108 These are the ones whose hearts, hearing and sight are sealed up by Allah[1]; it is they who are the heedless.

16:109 No doubt that it is they who will be the greatest losers in the Hereafter.

16:110 As for those who emigrated after being persecuted, then strove [for the cause of Allah], and were patient[1] – indeed, your Lord is after that All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.
