Rowwad Translation Center — 434

34:49 Say, “The Truth has come, and falsehood can neither initiate nor restore anything[1].”

34:50 Say, “If I go astray, the detriment of my straying is only upon myself; but if I am guided, it is because of what my Lord reveals to me. He is indeed All-Hearing, Ever Near.”

34:51 If only you could see when they are stricken with terror, but there will be no escape, and they will be seized from a nearby place[1].

34:52 They will say, “Now we believe in it,” but how could they attain faith from such a distant place[1],

34:53 while they disbelieved in it before, making far-fetched conjectures about the unseen[1]?

34:54 A barrier will be placed between them and what they desire[1], as was done with the likes of them before, for they were indeed in a disquieting doubt.

35:1 All praise be to Allah, the Originator of the heavens and earth, Who made the angels messengers with two, three, or four wings. He increases in creation whatever He wills. Indeed, Allah is Most Capable of all things.

35:2 Whatever mercy Allah opens up for people, none can withhold it; and whatever He withholds, none can release it thereafter, for He is the All-Mighty, All-Wise.

35:3 O people, remember Allah’s favor upon you. Is there any creator other than Allah who gives you provision from heaven and earth? None has the right to be worshiped except Him. How can you then be deluded?
