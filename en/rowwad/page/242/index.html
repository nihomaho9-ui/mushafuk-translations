Rowwad Translation Center — 242

12:53 I[1] do not absolve myself of blame, for Indeed the soul prompts one to evil, except those to whom my Lord may show mercy. Indeed, my Lord is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.”

12:54 The King said, “Bring him to me; I will appoint him [as an adviser] exclusively to myself.” Then when he spoke to him, he said, “You are today in a highly-esteemed position and fully trusted by us.”

12:55 Joseph said, “Put me in charge of the storehouses of the land[1]; I am indeed a good keeper and knowledgeable[2].”

12:56 This is how We established Joseph in the land[1] so that he may settle wherever he wished. We extend Our mercy to whom We will, and We do not let the reward of those who do good to go to waste.

12:57 But the reward of the Hereafter is better for those who believe and fear Allah.

12:58 The brothers of Joseph came and presented themselves before him; he recognized them but they did not know him.

12:59 When he prepared for them their rations, he said, “Bring me your brother on your father’s side[1]; do you not see that I give full measure and I am the best host?

12:60 If you do not bring him to me, you will never receive any measure [of grain] from me, nor will you even come close to me.”

12:61 They said, “We will persuade his father to send him[1], and we will surely do our best.”

12:62 Joseph said to his servants, “Put their trading goods back in their saddlebags so that they may find them when they go back to their family; perhaps they will return.”

12:63 When they returned to their father, they said, “O our father, we have been denied any further measure [of grain]; send our brother with us so that we may receive our measure, and we will surely protect him.”
