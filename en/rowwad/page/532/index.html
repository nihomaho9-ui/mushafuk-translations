Rowwad Translation Center — 532

55:19 He merges the two seas meeting together[1],

55:20 yet between them is a barrier so that they do not cross[1].

55:21 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:22 Out of both of them emerge pearls and coral.

55:23 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:24 To Him belong the sailing ships raised up in the sea like mountains.

55:25 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:26 Everyone on earth will perish,

55:27 but there will remain the Face of your Lord[1], full of Majesty and Honor.

55:28 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:29 All those in the heavens and earth ask of Him; every day He is engaged in some matters[1].

55:30 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:31 We will attend to you, O two multitudes [of men and jinn].

55:32 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:33 O assembly of jinn and mankind, if you are able to pass beyond the realms of the heavens and earth, then pass; you cannot pass without [Our] authority.

55:34 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:35 There will be sent against you a flame of fire and smoke[1], and you will not be able to defend yourselves.

55:36 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:37 When the heaven is split asunder and becomes crimson like [burnt] oil[1].

55:38 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:39 On that Day no human or jinn will be asked about his sin[1].

55:40 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:41 The wicked will be known by their marks and will be seized by their forelocks and their feet.
