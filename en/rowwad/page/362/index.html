Rowwad Translation Center — 362

25:21 Those who do not expect to meet Us say, “Why are angels not sent down to us or why do we not see our Lord?” They have been too proud of themselves, and have transgressed immensely.

25:22 On the Day when they see the angels[1], there will be no glad tidings for the wicked, and [the angels] will say, “Absolutely forbidden for you[2].”

25:23 We will turn to whatever deeds they did and turn them into scattered dust[1].

25:24 On that Day the people of Paradise will have the best abode and the finest resting place.

25:25 On the Day when the sky will split asunder with clouds, and the angels will be sent down in succession.

25:26 The true dominion on that Day belongs to the Most Compassionate[1], and it will be a hard day for the disbelievers.

25:27 On that Day, the wrongdoer will bite his hands saying, “Would that I had taken a way with the Messenger[1]!

25:28 Woe to me! Would that I had not taken so-and-so[1] as a close friend!

25:29 He led me astray from the Reminder[1] after it had come to me.” Satan always betrays man[2].

25:30 The Messenger said, “O my Lord, my people have taken this Qur’an as a thing to be abandoned[1].”

25:31 Thus did we assign for every prophet an enemy from among the wicked, but your Lord is sufficient as a Guide and Helper.

25:32 Those who disbelieve say, “Why was the Qur’an not sent down to him all at once?” [We sent it] in this way so that We may strengthen your heart thereby, and We have revealed it in a gradual manner.
