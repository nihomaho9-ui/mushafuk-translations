Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 569

70:11 11. Though they shall be made to see one another [(i.e. on the Day of Resurrection), there will be none but see his father, children and relatives, but he will neither speak to them nor will ask them for any help]. The Mujrim, (criminal, sinner, disbeliever) would desire to ransom himself from the punishment of that Day by his children,

70:12 12. And his wife and his brother,

70:13 13. And his kindred who sheltered him,

70:14 14. And all that are in the earth, so that it might save him.[1]

70:15 15. By no means! Verily, it will be the Fire of Hell,

70:16 16. Taking away (burning completely) the head skin!

70:17 17. Calling[2] (all) such as turn their backs and turn away their faces (from Faith) [picking and swallowing them up from that great gathering of mankind on the Day of Resurrection just as a bird picks up a food-grain from the earth with its beak and swallows it up] (Tafsir Al-Qurtubî)

70:18 18. And collect (wealth) and hide it (from spending it in the Cause of Allâh).

70:19 19. Verily, man (disbeliever) was created very impatient;

70:20 20. Irritable (discontented) when evil touches him;

70:21 21. And niggardly when good touches him.

70:22 22. Except those who are devoted to Salât (prayers).[3]

70:23 23. Those who remain constant in their Salât (prayers);

70:24 24. And those in whose wealth there is a recognised right

70:25 25. For the beggar who asks, and for the unlucky who has lost his property and wealth, (and his means of living has been straitened).

70:26 26. And those who believe in the Day of Recompense.

70:27 27. And those who fear the torment of their Lord.

70:28 28. Verily, the torment of their Lord is that before which none can feel secure. -

70:29 29. And those who guard their chastity (i.e. private parts from illegal sexual acts).[4]

70:30 30. Except from their wives or the (women slaves) whom their right hands possess - for (then) they are not blameworthy.

70:31 31. But whosoever seeks beyond that, then it is those who are trespassers.[5]

70:32 32. And those who keep their trusts and covenants.

70:33 33. And those who stand firm in their testimonies.

70:34 34. And those who guard their Salât (prayers) well.[6]

70:35 35. Such shall dwell in the Gardens (i.e. Paradise), honoured.

70:36 36. So what is the matter with those who disbelieve that they hasten to listen from you [O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم), in order to belie you and to mock at you, and at Allâh’s Book (this Qur’ân)].

70:37 37. (Sitting) in groups on the right and on the left (of you, O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم)?

70:38 38. Does every man of them hope to enter the Paradise of Delight?

70:39 39. No, that is not like that! Verily, We have created them out of that which they know!
