Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 521

51:7 7. By the heaven full of paths,

51:8 8. Certainly, you have different ideas (about Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم and the Qur’ân).

51:9 9. Turned aside therefrom (i.e. from Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم and the Qur’ân) is he who is turned aside (by the Decree and Preordainment القضاء والقدر of Allâh).

51:10 10. Cursed be the liars

51:11 11. Who are under a cover of heedlessness (think not about the gravity of the Hereafter),

51:12 12. They ask: "When will be the Day of Recompense?"

51:13 13. (It will be) a Day when they will be tried (punished i.e. burnt) over the Fire!

51:14 14. "Taste you your trial (punishment i.e. burning)! This is what you used to ask to be hastened!"

51:15 15. Verily, the Muttaqûn (the pious. See V.2:2) will be in the midst of Gardens and Springs (in the Paradise),

51:16 16. Taking joy in the things which their Lord has given them. Verily, they were before this Muhsinûn (good-doers. See V.2:112).

51:17 17. They used to sleep but little by night [invoking their Lord (Allâh) and praying, with fear and hope].

51:18 18. And in the hours before dawn, they were (found) asking (Allâh) for forgiveness.

51:19 19. And in their properties there was the right of the Sâ’il (the beggar who asks) and the Mahrûm (the poor who does not ask others)[1].

51:20 20. And on the earth are signs for those who have Faith with certainty.

51:21 21. And also in your ownselves. Will you not then see?

51:22 22. And in the heaven is your provision, and that which you are promised.

51:23 23. Then by the Lord of the heaven and the earth, it is the truth (i.e. what has been promised to you), just as it is the truth that you can speak.

51:24 24. Has the story reached you, of the honoured guests [three angels; Jibrîl (Gabriel) along with another two] of Ibrâhîm (Abraham)?

51:25 25. When they came in to him and said: "Salâm, (peace be upon you)!" He answered: "Salâm, (peace be upon you)," and said: "You are a people unknown to me."

51:26 26. Then he turned to his household, and brought out a roasted calf [as the property of Ibrâhîm (Abraham) was mainly cows].

51:27 27. And placed it before them, (saying): "Will you not eat?"

51:28 28. Then he conceived fear of them (when they ate not). They said: "Fear not."[2] And they gave him glad tidings of a son having knowledge (about Allâh and His religion of True Monotheism).

51:29 29. Then his wife came forward with a loud voice: she smote her face, and said: "A barren old woman!"[3]

51:30 30. They said: "Even so says your Lord.[4] Verily, He is the All-Wise, the All-Knower."
