Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 596

92:10 10. We will make smooth for him the path for evil.

92:11 11. And what will his wealth avail him when he goes down (in destruction)?.

92:12 12. Truly! On Us is (to give) guidance.

92:13 13. And truly, unto Us (belong) the last (Hereafter) and the first (this world).

92:14 14. Therefore I have warned you of a blazing Fire (Hell).

92:15 15. None shall enter it save the most wretched.

92:16 16. Who denies and turns away.

92:17 17. And Al-Muttaqûn (the pious - See V.2:2) will be far removed from it (Hell).

92:18 18. He who spends his wealth for increase in self-purification,

92:19 19. And who has (in mind) no favour from anyone to be paid back,

92:20 20. Except to seek the Countenance of his Lord, the Most High.

92:21 21. He surely will be pleased (when he enters Paradise).

93:1 1. By the forenoon (after sunrise).[1]

93:2 2. By the night when it darkens (and stand still).

93:3 3. Your Lord (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) has neither forsaken you nor hates you.

93:4 4. And indeed the Hereafter is better for you than the present (life of this world).

93:5 5. And verily, your Lord will give you (all good) so that you shall be well-pleased.

93:6 6. Did He not find you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) an orphan and gave you a refuge?

93:7 7. And He found you unaware (of the Qur’ân, its laws, and Prophethood) and guided you?

93:8 8. And He found you poor and made you rich (self-sufficient with self-contentment)?

93:9 9. Therefore, treat not the orphan with oppression.

93:10 10. And repulse not the beggar.

93:11 11. And proclaim the Grace of your Lord (i.e. the Prophethood and all other Graces).

94:1 1. Have We not opened your breast for you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم)?

94:2 2. And removed from you your burden.
