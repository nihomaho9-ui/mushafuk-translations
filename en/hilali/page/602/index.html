Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 602

106:1 1. (It is a great Grace from Allâh) for the protection of the Quraish,

106:2 2. (And with all those Allâh’s Grace and Protections, We cause) the (Quraish) caravans to set forth safe in winter (to the south) and in summer (to the north without any fear),

106:3 3. So let them worship (Allâh) the Lord of this House (the Ka‘bah in Makkah),

106:4 4. (He) Who has fed them against hunger, and has made them safe from fear.

107:1 1. Have you seen him who denies the Recompense?

107:2 2. That is he who repulses the orphan (harshly),[1]

107:3 3. And urges not on the feeding of Al-Miskîn (the poor),[2]

107:4 4. So woe unto those performers of Salât (prayers) (hypocrites),

107:5 5. Those who delay their Salât (prayer from their stated fixed times).

107:6 6. Those who do good deeds only to be seen (of men),

107:7 7. And prevent Al-Mâ‘ûn (small kindnesses like salt, sugar, water).

108:1 1. Verily, We have granted you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) Al-Kauthar (a river in Paradise).[1]

108:2 2. Therefore turn in prayer to your Lord and sacrifice (to Him only).

108:3 3. For he who hates you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم), he will be cut off (from posterity and every good thing in this world and in the Hereafter).[2]
