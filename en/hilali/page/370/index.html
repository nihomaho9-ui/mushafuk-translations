Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 370

26:61 61. And when the two hosts saw each other, the companions of Mûsâ (Moses) said: "We are sure to be overtaken."

26:62 62. [Mûsâ (Moses)] said: "Nay, verily with me is my Lord. He will guide me."

26:63 63. Then We revealed to Mûsâ (Moses) (saying): "Strike the sea with your stick." And it parted, and each separate part (of that sea water) became like huge mountain.

26:64 64. Then We brought near the others [Fir‘aun’s (Pharaoh) party] to that place.

26:65 65. And We saved Mûsâ (Moses) and all those with him.

26:66 66. Then We drowned the others.

26:67 67. Verily in this is indeed a sign (or a proof), yet most of them are not believers.

26:68 68. And verily your Lord, He is truly the All-Mighty, the Most Merciful.

26:69 69. And recite to them the story of Ibrâhîm (Abraham).

26:70 70. When he said to his father and his people: "What do you worship?"

26:71 71. They said: "We worship idols, and to them we are ever devoted."

26:72 72. He said: "Do they hear you, when you call on (them)?

26:73 73. "Or do they benefit you or do they harm (you)?"

26:74 74. They said: "(Nay) but we found our fathers doing so."

26:75 75. He said: "Do you observe that which you have been worshipping -

26:76 76. "You and your ancient fathers?

26:77 77. "Verily they are enemies to me, save the Lord of the ‘Âlamîn (mankind, jinn and all that exists),

26:78 78. "Who has created me, and it is He Who guides me.

26:79 79. "And it is He Who feeds me and gives me to drink.

26:80 80. "And when I am ill, it is He who cures me.

26:81 81. "And Who will cause me to die, and then will bring me to life (again).

26:82 82. "And Who, I hope, will forgive me my faults on the Day of Recompense, (the Day of Resurrection).

26:83 83. My Lord! Bestow Hukm (religious knowledge, right judgement of the affairs and Prophethood) on me, and join me with the righteous.
