Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 445

36:71 71. Do they not see that We have created for them of what Our Hands have created the cattle, so that they are their owners.

36:72 72. And We have subdued them unto them so that some of them they have for riding and some they eat.

36:73 73. And they have (other) benefits from them, and they get (milk) to drink. Will they not then be grateful?

36:74 74. And they have taken besides Allâh âlihah (gods), hoping that they might be helped (by those so-called gods).

36:75 75. They cannot help them, but they will be brought forward as a troop against those who worshipped them (at the time of Reckoning).

36:76 76. So let not their speech, then, grieve you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم). Verily, We know what they conceal and what they reveal.

36:77 77. Does not man see that We have created him from Nutfah (mixed male and female sexual discharge - semen drops). Yet behold he (stands forth) as an open opponent.

36:78 78. And he puts forth for Us a parable, and forgets his own creation. He says: "Who will give life to these bones after they are rotten and have become dust?"

36:79 79. Say: (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) "He will give life to them Who created them for the first time! And He is the All-Knower of every creation!"

36:80 80. He Who produces for you fire out of the green tree, when behold you kindle therewith.

36:81 81. Is not He Who created the heavens and the earth, Able to create the like of them? Yes, indeed! He is the All-Knowing Supreme Creator.

36:82 82. Verily, His Command, when He intends a thing, is only that He says to it, "Be!" - and it is!

36:83 83. So glorified be He and exalted above all that they associate with Him, and in Whose Hands is the dominion of all things: and to Him you shall be returned.
