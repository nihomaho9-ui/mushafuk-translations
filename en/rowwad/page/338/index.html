Rowwad Translation Center — 338

22:47 They ask you to hasten the punishment, but Allah will not fail in His promise. A single day with your Lord is like one thousand years by your count.

22:48 How many towns did I give respite when they were wrongdoers, and then I seized them. And to Me is the final return.

22:49 Say, “O people, I am only sent to you as a clear warner.

22:50 Those who believe and do righteous deeds will have forgiveness and a generous provision.

22:51 But those who strive to discredit Our verses[1], they are the people of the Blazing Fire.”

22:52 We did not send any messenger or prophet before you except that when he recited, Satan would cast into it [some misunderstanding]. But Allah would abolish what Satan casts, then Allah would confirm His verses[1], for Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise.

22:53 [That is] so that He may make what Satan casts a trial for those [hypocrites] whose hearts are sick[1] and those [disbelievers] whose hearts are hardened, for indeed the wrongdoers are in extreme dissension.

22:54 And so that those who were given knowledge may know that this [Qur’an] is the truth from your Lord, so they may believe in it and their hearts may become humbled to it. Allah surely guides those who believe to the straight path.

22:55 Yet the disbelievers will persist in doubt about it until the Hour comes upon them suddenly or there comes upon them the punishment of a barren Day[1].
