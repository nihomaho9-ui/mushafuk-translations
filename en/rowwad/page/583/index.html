Rowwad Translation Center — 583

78:31 Indeed, the righteous will have salvation[1],

78:32 gardens and vineyards,

78:33 and full-bosomed maidens of equal age,

78:34 and a full cup [of wine][1].

78:35 They will not hear therein vain talk or lies –

78:36 a reward and a generous gift from your Lord[1],

78:37 [from] the Lord of the heavens and earth and all that is between them, the Most Compassionate; none will dare to speak to Him[1].

78:38 On the Day when the Spirit and the angels will stand in rows; none will dare to speak, except those to whom the Most Compassionate granted permission, and they will only speak the truth.

78:39 That Day is sure to come. So whoever wills may seek a path leading to his Lord[1].

78:40 Indeed, We have warned you of an imminent punishment on the Day when everyone will see what his hands have sent forth[1], and the disbeliever will say, “Oh, I wish that I were dust!”

79:1 By those who pull out [evil souls][1] harshly,

79:2 and by those who draw out [good souls][1] gently,

79:3 and by those who glide swiftly[1],

79:4 and those who overtake one another as in a race[1],

79:5 and those who carry out the command [of Allah],

79:6 on the Day when the earth convulses [by the first Blast],

79:7 followed by the second Blast,

79:8 hearts[1] will be pounding on that Day,

79:9 and their eyes[1] will be downcast.

79:10 They say, “Will we be brought back to life,

79:11 after we have turned into decayed bones[1]?”

79:12 They say, “Then that would surely be a losing return[1]!”

79:13 It will only be a single Blast,

79:14 then they will immediately be above the ground.

79:15 Has there come to you the story of Moses,

79:16 when his Lord called out to him at the sacred valley of Tuwa?
