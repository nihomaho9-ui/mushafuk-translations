Rowwad Translation Center — 212

10:26 For those who do good, there will be the best reward and more[1] – neither gloom nor humiliation will cover their faces[2]. It is they who are the people of Paradise, they will abide therein forever.

10:27 As for those who commit evil deeds, the recompense of an evil deed is the like thereof, and humiliation will cover them[1] – there will be none to protect them from Allah – as if their faces were covered with patches of the darkest night. It is they who are the people of the Fire, they will abide therein forever.

10:28 On the Day when We gather them all together, then We will say to those who associated partners with Us, “Stay where you are, you and your [so-called] partners!” Then We will separate them, and their partners[1] will say, “It was not us that you used to worship!

10:29 Allah is sufficient as a Witness between us and you that we were completely unaware of your worship.”

10:30 Thereupon, every soul will realize what it did in the past. They will be brought back to Allah, their True Guardian, and all [false gods] that they fabricated will vanish from them.

10:31 Say, “Who provides for you from the heaven and earth? Or who owns [your] hearing and sight? Who brings forth the living from the dead and the dead from the living? Who controls all things?” They will say, “Allah[1].” Say, “Do you not then fear Him?

10:32 Such is Allah, your True Lord. What is beyond the truth except falsehood? So how could you be averted [from the truth]?”

10:33 Thus the decree of your Lord has come true against the wicked, that they will never believe[1].
