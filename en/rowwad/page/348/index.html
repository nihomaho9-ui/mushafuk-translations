Rowwad Translation Center — 348

23:90 In fact, We brought them the truth, but they are certainly liars[1].

23:91 Allah has never begotten a son, nor is there any god besides Him. Otherwise, each god would have taken away what he had created, and each would have tried to gain supremacy over the other[1]. Glory be to Allah far above what they ascribe to Him!

23:92 Knower of the unseen and the seen. Exalted is He far above what they associate with Him!

23:93 Say, “My Lord, if You show me what they[1] are warned of,

23:94 my Lord, then do not include me among the wrongdoing people[1].”

23:95 We are surely able to show you that of which We have warned them.

23:96 Repel evil with what is best; We know well what they claim[1].

23:97 And say, “My Lord, I seek refuge with You from the temptations of the devils.

23:98 And I seek refuge with You, my Lord, lest they come near me.”

23:99 Until when death comes to one of them, he says, “My Lord, send me back,

23:100 so that I may do righteous deeds in what I left behind.” No, it is only a word he utters. And behind them is a barrier until the Day they are resurrected[1]

23:101 When the Trumpet is blown[1], there will be no kinship ties between them on that Day, nor will they ask about one another[2].

23:102 So those whose scales [of good deeds] are heavy, it is they who will be successful.

23:103 But those whose scales are light, they are the ones who have lost their souls, abiding in Hell forever.

23:104 The Fire will scorch their faces, leaving them disfigured[1].
