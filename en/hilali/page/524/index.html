Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 524

52:15 15. Is this magic, or do you not see?

52:16 16. Taste you therein its heat and whether you are patient of it or impatient of it, it is all the same. You are only being requited for what you used to do.

52:17 17. Verily, the Muttaqûn (the pious. See V.2:2) will be in Gardens (Paradise), and Delight.

52:18 18. Enjoying in that which their Lord has bestowed on them, and (the fact that) their Lord saved them from the torment of the blazing Fire.

52:19 19. "Eat and drink with happiness because of what you used to do."

52:20 20. They will recline (with ease) on thrones arranged in ranks. And We shall marry them to Hûr: (female, fair ones) with wide lovely eyes.[4]

52:21 21. And those who believe and whose offspring follow them in Faith: to them shall We join their offspring, and We shall not decrease the reward of their deeds in anything. Every person is a pledge for that which he has earned.

52:22 22. And We shall provide them with fruit and meat such as they desire.

52:23 23. There they shall pass from hand to hand a (wine) cup, free from any Laghw (dirty, false, evil vain talk between them), and free from sin[5] (because it will be lawful for them to drink).

52:24 24. And there will go round boy-servants of theirs, to serve them as if they were preserved pearls.

52:25 25. And some of them draw near to others, questioning.

52:26 26. Saying: "Aforetime, we were afraid (of the punishment of Allâh) in the midst of our families.

52:27 27. "So Allâh has been gracious to us, and has saved us from the torment of the Fire.

52:28 28. "Verily, We used to invoke Him (Alone and none else) before. Verily, He is Al-Barr (the Most Subtle, Kind, Courteous, and Generous), the Most Merciful."[6]

52:29 29. Therefore, remind (mankind of Islâmic Monotheism, O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم). By the Grace of Allâh, you are neither a soothsayer, nor a madman.

52:30 30. Or do they say: "(Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) is a poet! We await for him some calamity by time!"

52:31 31. Say (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم to them): "Wait! I am with you among the waiters!"
