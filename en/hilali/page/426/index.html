Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 426

33:55 55. It is no sin on them (the Prophet’s wives, if they appear unveiled) before their fathers, or their sons, or their brothers, or their brother’s sons, or the sons of their sisters, or their own (believing) women, or their (female) slaves. And (O ladies), fear (keep your duty to) Allâh. Verily, Allâh is Ever All-Witness over everything.

33:56 56. Allâh sends His Salât (Graces, Honours, Blessings, Mercy) on the Prophet (Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم), and also His angels (ask Allâh to bless and forgive him). O you who believe! Send your Salât[11] on (ask Allâh to bless) him (Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم), and (you should) greet (salute) him with the Islâmic way of greeting (salutation i.e. As-Salâmu ‘Alaikum ).

33:57 57. Verily, those who annoy Allâh and His Messenger (صلى الله عليه وسلم)[12] Allâh has cursed them in this world, and in the Hereafter, and has prepared for them a humiliating torment.

33:58 58. And those who annoy believing men and women undeservedly, they bear (on themselves) the crime of slander and plain sin.

33:59 59. O Prophet! Tell your wives and your daughters and the women of the believers to draw their cloaks (veils) all over their bodies (i.e. screen themselves completely except the eyes or one eye to see the way). That will be better, that they should be known (as free respectable women) so as not to be annoyed. And Allâh is Ever Oft-Forgiving, Most Merciful.

33:60 60. If the hypocrites, and those in whose hearts is a disease (evil desire for illegal sex), and those who spread false news among the people in Al-Madinah stop not, We shall certainly let you overpower them: then they will not be able to stay in it as your neighbours but a little while.

33:61 61. Accursed, they shall be seized wherever found, and killed with a (terrible) slaughter.

33:62 62. That was the Way of Allâh in the case of those who passed away of old: and you will not find any change in the Way of Allâh.
