Rowwad Translation Center — 1

1:1 In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful.

1:2 All praise be to Allah[1], the Lord[2] of the worlds[3],

1:3 the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful[1],

1:4 Master[1] of the Day of Judgment.

1:5 You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help.

1:6 Guide us to the straight path,

1:7 the path of those whom You have blessed[1]; not of those who incurred Your Wrath[2], or of those who went astray[3].
