Rowwad Translation Center — 85

4:38 And [also] those who spend their wealth to show off to people, but do not believe in Allah or the Last Day. Whoever[1] takes Satan as an associate has indeed taken an evil associate.

4:39 What harm would it do to them to believe in Allah and the Last Day, and spend out of what Allah has provided for them? For Allah is All-Knowing of them.

4:40 Allah does not do injustice as much as an atom’s weight[1]. And if it is a good deed, He will multiply it and give from Himself a great reward.

4:41 How will it be when We bring a witness from every community, and bring you [O Prophet] as a witness against these [people]?

4:42 On that Day, those who disbelieved and disobeyed the Messenger will wish that the earth were leveled upon them. And they will not be able to hide anything from Allah[1].

4:43 O you who believe, do not approach the prayer while you are intoxicated[1] until you know what you are saying, nor in a state of major impurity – except while passing through [a mosque] – until you take a bath. If you are ill, or on a journey, or have relieved yourselves, or had intercourse with [your] women and find no water, then purify yourselves with clean earth and wipe your faces and hands with it. For Allah is Ever-Pardoning, All-Forgiving.

4:44 Have you not seen those who were given a portion of the Book – purchasing misguidance and wishing that you lose your way?
