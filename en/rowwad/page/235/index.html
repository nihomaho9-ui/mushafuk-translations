Rowwad Translation Center — 235

11:118 If your Lord had willed, He could have made mankind a single community, but they will not cease to differ[1],

11:119 except those whom Allah has given mercy[1], and for this He created them[2], and the word of your Lord will be fulfilled: “I will certainly fill Hell with jinn and humans all together.”

11:120 We relate to you [O Prophet] the stories of the messengers to strengthen your heart. And there has come to you in this the truth, an admonition and a reminder to the believers.

11:121 Say to those who do not believe, “Carry on in your ways; so will we do.

11:122 And wait; we too are waiting.”

11:123 To Allah belongs the unseen of the heavens and earth, and to Him will return all matters. So worship Him and put your trust in Him, for your Lord is not unaware of what you do.

12:1 Alif Lām Ra[1]. These are the verses of the clear Book.

12:2 We have sent it down as an Arabic Qur’an so that you may understand.

12:3 We relate to you [O Prophet] the best of stories through Our revelation of this Qur’an, although before this you were unaware of them.

12:4 [Remember] when Joseph said to his father[1], “O my father, I saw [in a dream] eleven stars, the sun and the moon – I saw them prostrating to me.”
