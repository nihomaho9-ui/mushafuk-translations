74:48 48. So no intercession of intercessors will be of any use to them.

74:49 49. Then what is wrong with them (i.e. the polytheists, the disbelievers) that they turn away from (receiving) admonition?

74:50 50. As if they were (frightened) wild donkeys.

74:51 51. Fleeing from a hunter, or a lion, or a beast of prey.

74:52 52. Nay, everyone of them desires that he should be given pages spread out (coming from Allâh with a writing that Islâm is the right religion, and Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has come with the truth from Allâh, the Lord of the heavens and earth).

74:53 53. Nay! But they fear not the Hereafter (from Allâh’s punishment).

74:54 54. Nay, verily, this (Qur’an) is an admonition,

74:55 55. So whosoever will (let him read it), and receive admonition (from it)!

74:56 56. And they will not receive admonition unless Allâh wills; He (Allâh) is the One, deserving that mankind should be afraid of, and should be dutiful to Him, and should not take any Ilâh (god) along with Him, and He is the One Who forgives (sins).

75:1 1. I swear by the Day of Resurrection.

75:2 2. And I swear by the self-reproaching person (a believer).

75:3 3. Does man (a disbeliever) think that We shall not assemble his bones?

75:4 4. Yes, We are Able to put together in perfect order the tips of his fingers.[1]

75:5 5. Nay! Man (denies Resurrection and Reckoning. So he) desires to continue committing sins.

75:6 6. He asks: "When will be this Day of Resurrection?"

75:7 7. So, when the sight shall be dazed.

75:8 8. And the moon will be eclipsed.

75:9 9. And the sun and moon will be joined together (by going one into the other or folded up or deprived of their light).[2]

75:10 10. On that Day man will say: "Where (is the refuge) to flee?"

75:11 11. No! There is no refuge!

75:12 12. Unto your Lord (Alone) will be the place of rest that Day.

75:13 13. On that Day man will be informed of what he sent forward (of his evil good deeds), and what he left behind (of his good or evil traditions).

75:14 14. Nay! Man will be a witness against himself [as his body parts (skin, hands, legs, etc.) will speak about his deeds],

75:15 15. Though he may put forth his excuses (to cover his evil deeds).

75:16 16. Move not your tongue concerning (the Qur’ân, O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) to make haste therewith.

75:17 17. It is for Us to collect it and to give you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) the ability to recite it (the Qur’ân).

75:18 18. And when We have recited it to you [O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم through Jibrîl (Gabriel)], then follow its (the Qur’an’s) recital.

75:19 19. Then it is for Us (Allâh) to make it clear (to you).
