Rowwad Translation Center — 407

30:25 And among His signs is that the sky and earth are maintained by His command. Then when He calls you out of the earth, you will immediately come forth.

30:26 To Him belong all those who are in the heavens and earth; all are devotedly obedient to Him.

30:27 It is He Who originates the creation and then repeats it, which is easier for Him. To Him belong the most sublime attributes in the heavens and earth, and He is the All-Mighty, the All-Wise.

30:28 He makes this comparison for you from your own lives: do you have among your slaves any partners who equally share with you whatever wealth we have given you[1], so you are fearing them as you fear one another[2]? This is how We make the verses clear for people who understand.

30:29 In fact, the wrongdoers follow their own desires without any knowledge. Who can guide those whom Allah caused to stray? They will have no helpers.

30:30 Adhere sincerely to the true religion in all uprightness. This is the natural disposition[1] with which Allah has created mankind. There is no change in Allah’s creation. This is the straight religion, but most people do not know[2].

30:31 Turn to Him [in repentance] and fear Him, and establish prayer, and do not be among those who associate partners with Allah,

30:32 those who split up their religion and became sects, each party rejoicing in what it has[1].
