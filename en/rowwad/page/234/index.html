Rowwad Translation Center — 234

11:109 Have no doubt about what they worship; they only worship what their forefathers worshiped. We will surely give them their full share undiminished.

11:110 We gave Moses the Scripture, but a dispute arose about it. If it had not been for a prior decree from your Lord, the matter would have been decided between them[1]. They are indeed in a disquieting doubt about it.

11:111 Your Lord will surely pay everyone in full for their deeds, for He is All-Aware of what they do.

11:112 So stay on a straight path as you have been commanded, along with those who repented with you, and do not cross the limits[1]. Indeed, He is All-Seeing of what you do.

11:113 Do not incline to the wrongdoers, or else the Fire will seize you. Then you will have no protectors besides Allah, nor will you be helped.

11:114 Establish prayer at the two ends of the day and at some hours of the night. Indeed, good deeds wipe out evil deeds[1]. This is a reminder for those who are mindful.

11:115 And be patient, for indeed Allah does not let the reward of those who do good go to waste.

11:116 If only there had been among the generations before you righteous people who would forbid others from spreading corruption in the land, except a few of them whom We saved. But the wrongdoers pursued their luxury[1] which they were given therein, and they were wicked.

11:117 Your Lord would never destroy the towns unjustly while their people were acting righteously.
