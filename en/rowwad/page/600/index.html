Rowwad Translation Center — 600

100:6 indeed, man is very ungrateful to his Lord[1] –

100:7 and he himself is a witness to that[1] –

100:8 and he is truly extreme in his love of wealth.

100:9 Does he not know that when the contents of graves will be overturned,

100:10 and the secrets of the hearts will be brought to light[1]?

100:11 Indeed, their Lord is All-Aware of them on that Day[1].

101:1 The Striking Calamity!

101:2 What is the Striking Calamity?

101:3 How do you know what the Striking Calamity is?

101:4 On that Day people will be like scattered moths[1],

101:5 and the mountains will be like carded wool[1].

101:6 Then the one whose scales of good deeds are heavy,

101:7 will have a pleasant life[1].

101:8 But the one whose scales of good deeds are light,

101:9 his abode will be the abyss[1].

101:10 And how do you know what it is?

101:11 It is a Blazing Fire.

102:1 Competition for worldly gains distracts you [from Allah],

102:2 until you come to your graves.

102:3 No indeed! You will come to know.

102:4 Again no! You will come to know.

102:5 No indeed! If only you knew for certain[1].

102:6 You will surely see the Blazing Fire,

102:7 Again, you will see it with absolute certainty.

102:8 Then on that Day you will surely be asked about your worldly pleasures[1].
