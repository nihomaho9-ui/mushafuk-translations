Rowwad Translation Center — 2

2:1 Alif Lām Mīm[1].

2:2 This is the Book about which there is no doubt, a guidance for the righteous[1],

2:3 those who believe in the unseen, establish prayer[1], and spend out of what We[2] have provided for them,

2:4 and those who believe in what has been sent down to you [O Prophet] and in what was sent down before you[1], and in the Hereafter they believe with certainty.

2:5 It is they who are upon guidance from their Lord, and it is they who are successful.
