19:96 96. Verily, those who believe [in the Oneness of Allâh and in His Messenger (Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم)] and work deeds of righteousness, the Most Gracious (Allâh) will bestow love for them[15] (in the hearts of the believers).

19:97 97. So We have made this (the Qur’ân) easy in your own tongue (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم), only that you may give glad tidings to the Muttaqûn (the pious - See V.2:2), and warn with it the Ludd[16] (most quarrelsome) people.

19:98 98. And how many a generation before them have We destroyed! Can you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) find a single one of them or hear even a whisper of them?

20:1 1. Tâ-Hâ.

20:2 2. We have not sent down the Qur’ân unto you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) to cause you distress,

20:3 3. But only as a Reminder to those who fear (Allâh).

20:4 4. A revelation from Him (Allâh) Who has created the earth and high heavens.

20:5 5. The Most Gracious (Allâh) rose over (Istawâ) the (Mighty) Throne (in a manner that suits His Majesty).

20:6 6. To Him belongs all that is in the heavens and all that is on the earth, and all that is between them, and all that is under the soil.

20:7 7. And if you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) speak (the invocation) aloud, then verily, He knows the secret and that which is yet more hidden.

20:8 8. Allâh! Lâ ilâha illa Huwa (none has the right to be worshipped but He)! To Him belong the Best Names.[1]

20:9 9. And has there come to you the story of Mûsâ (Moses)?

20:10 10. When he saw a fire, he said to his family: "Wait! Verily, I have seen a fire; perhaps I can bring you some burning brand therefrom, or find some guidance at the fire."

20:11 11. And when he came to it (the fire), he was called by name: "O Mûsâ (Moses)!

20:12 12. "Verily I am your Lord! So take off your shoes; you are in the sacred valley, Tuwa.
