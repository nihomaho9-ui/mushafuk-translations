تَرْهَقُهَا قَتَرَةٌ ﴿41﴾

أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْكَفَرَةُ ٱلْفَجَرَةُ ﴿42﴾

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ إِذَا ٱلشَّمْسُ كُوِّرَتْ ﴿1﴾

وَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ ٱنكَدَرَتْ ﴿2﴾

وَإِذَا ٱلْوُحُوشُ حُشِرَتْ ﴿5﴾

وَإِذَا ٱلْبِحَارُ سُجِّرَتْ ﴿6﴾

وَإِذَا ٱلنُّفُوسُ زُوِّجَتْ ﴿7﴾

وَإِذَا ٱلْمَوْءُۥدَةُ سُئِلَتْ ﴿8﴾

بِأَىِّ ذَنۢبٍ قُتِلَتْ ﴿9﴾

وَإِذَا ٱلصُّحُفُ نُشِرَتْ ﴿10﴾

وَإِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ كُشِطَتْ ﴿11﴾

وَإِذَا ٱلْجَحِيمُ سُعِّرَتْ ﴿12﴾

عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌ مَّآ أَحْضَرَتْ ﴿14﴾

فَلَآ أُقْسِمُ بِٱلْخُنَّسِ ﴿15﴾

ٱلْجَوَارِ ٱلْكُنَّسِ ﴿16﴾

وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا عَسْعَسَ ﴿17﴾

مُّطَاعٍ ثَمَّ أَمِينٍ ﴿21﴾

وَمَا صَاحِبُكُم بِمَجْنُونٍ ﴿22﴾

وَلَقَدْ رَءَاهُ بِٱلْأُفُقِ ٱلْمُبِينِ ﴿23﴾

وَمَا هُوَ عَلَى ٱلْغَيْبِ بِضَنِينٍ ﴿24﴾

وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوْلِ شَيْطَـٰنٍ رَّجِيمٍ ﴿25﴾

فَأَيْنَ تَذْهَبُونَ ﴿26﴾

إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكْرٌ لِّلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ﴿27﴾

لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمْ أَن يَسْتَقِيمَ ﴿28﴾

وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ﴿29﴾

Saheeh International

80:41 Blackness will cover them.

80:42 Those are the disbelievers, the wicked ones.

81:1 When the sun is wrapped up [in darkness]

81:2 And when the stars fall, dispersing,

81:3 And when the mountains are removed

81:4 And when full-term she-camels[1865] are neglected

81:5 And when the wild beasts are gathered

81:6 And when the seas are filled with flame[1866]

81:7 And when the souls are paired[1867]

81:8 And when the girl [who was] buried alive is asked

81:9 For what sin she was killed

81:10 And when the pages[1868] are spread [i.e., made public]

81:11 And when the sky is stripped away

81:12 And when Hellfire is set ablaze

81:13 And when Paradise is brought near,

81:14 A soul will [then] know what it has brought [with it].[1869]

81:15 So I swear by the retreating stars -

81:16 Those that run [their courses] and disappear [i.e., set]

81:17 And by the night as it closes in[1870]

81:18 And by the dawn when it breathes [i.e., stirs]

81:19 [That] indeed, it [i.e., the Qur’ān] is a word [conveyed by] a noble messenger [i.e., Gabriel]

81:20 [Who is] possessed of power and with the Owner of the Throne, secure [in position],

81:21 Obeyed there [in the heavens] and trustworthy.

81:22 And your companion [i.e., Prophet Muḥammad (ﷺ) ] is not [at all] mad.[1871]

81:23 And he has already seen him [i.e., Gabriel] in the clear horizon.[1872]

81:24 And he [i.e., Muḥammad (ﷺ)] is not a withholder of [knowledge of] the unseen.[1873]

81:25 And it [i.e., the Qur’ān] is not the word of a devil, expelled [from the heavens].

81:26 So where are you going?[1874]

81:27 It is not except a reminder to the worlds

81:28 For whoever wills among you to take a right course.

81:29 And you do not will except that Allāh wills - Lord of the worlds.
