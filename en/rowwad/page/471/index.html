Rowwad Translation Center — 471

40:34 Joseph came to you[1] before with clear proofs, but you never ceased to doubt what he brought you. Then when he died, you said, ‘Allah will never send a messenger after him.’ This is how Allah causes to stray anyone who transgresses and is given to much doubting,

40:35 those who argue against Allah’s signs with no proof given to them. How loathsome is that to Allah and to those who believe! This is how Allah seals the heart of every arrogant tyrant.”

40:36 Pharaoh said, “O Hāmān, build a tower for me so that I may reach the pathways[1] –

40:37 the pathways to the heavens so that I have a look at the God of Moses, for I think he is a liar.” This is how Pharaoh’s evil deeds were made appealing to him and he was hindered from the [right] way, and the plan of Pharaoh led only to ruin.

40:38 The man who believed said, “O my people, follow me, I will guide you to the right way.

40:39 O my people, the life of this world is only a brief enjoyment, whereas the Hereafter is the permanent abode.

40:40 Whoever does an evil deed will only be recompensed with the like thereof, but whoever does a righteous deed, whether male or female, and he is a believer – it is they who will enter Paradise, wherein they will be given provision without measure.
