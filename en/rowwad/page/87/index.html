Rowwad Translation Center — 87

4:52 It is they whom Allah has cursed, and whoever Allah curses, you will never find any helper for him.

4:53 Do they have a share in the dominion? Even if so, they would not give people even as much as the speck on a date stone.

4:54 Or do they envy people for what Allah has given them[1] out of His bounty? We gave the descendants of Abraham the Book and wisdom[2], and We gave them great authority[3].

4:55 Some of them believed in him [i.e., Muhammad], while others turned away from him. Hell is sufficient as a blaze.

4:56 Those who reject Our verses, We will cast them into the Fire. Whenever their skins are burnt through, We will replace them with fresh skins, so that they may taste the punishment. Indeed, Allah is All-Mighty, All-Wise.

4:57 As for those who believe and do righteous deeds, We will admit them into gardens under which rivers flow, abiding therein forever. They will have therein pure spouses[1], and We will admit them into a cool refreshing shade.

4:58 Indeed, Allah commands you to return trusts to their owners, and when you judge between people, judge with justice. What an excellent exhortation from Allah to you! Indeed, Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing.

4:59 O you who believe, obey Allah and obey the Messenger, and those of you who are in authority[1]. If you disagree over anything, refer it to Allah and the Messenger, if you believe in Allah and the Last Day; that is better and more commendable in the end.
