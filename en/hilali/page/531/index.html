Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 531

54:50 50. And Our Commandment is but one as the twinkling of an eye.

54:51 51. And indeed, We have destroyed your likes; then is there any that will remember (or receive admonition)?

54:52 52. And everything they have done is noted in (their) Records (of deeds).

54:53 53. And everything, small and big, is written down (in Al-Lauh Al-Mahfûz already beforehand i.e. before it befalls, or is done by its doer: الإيمان بالقدر) (See the Qur’ân V.57:22 and its footnote).

54:54 54. Verily, The Muttaqûn (the pious - see V.2:2), will be in the midst of Gardens and Rivers (Paradise).

54:55 55. In a seat of truth (i.e. Paradise), near the Omnipotent King (Allâh, the One, the All-Blessed, the Most High, the Owner of Majesty and Honour).

55:1 1. The Most Gracious (Allâh)!

55:2 2. He has taught (you mankind) the Qur’ân (by His Mercy).

55:3 3. He created man.

55:4 4. He taught him eloquent speech.

55:5 5. The sun and the moon run on their fixed courses (exactly) calculated with measured out stages for each (for reckoning).

55:6 6. And the herbs (or stars) and the trees both prostrate themselves (to Allah - See V.22:18) (Tafsir Ibn Kathir)

55:7 7. And the heaven: He has raised it high, and He has set up the Balance.

55:8 8. In order that you may not transgress (due) balance.

55:9 9. And observe the weight with equity and do not make the balance deficient.

55:10 10. And the earth: He has put down (laid) for the creatures.

55:11 11. Therein are fruits and date-palms producing sheathed fruit-stalks (enclosing dates).

55:12 12. And also corn, with (its) leaves and stalk for fodder, and sweet-scented plants.

55:13 13. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:14 14. He created man (Adam) from sounding clay like the clay of pottery.

55:15 15. And the jinn: He created from a smokeless flame of fire.

55:16 16. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:17 17. (He is) the Lord of the two easts (places of sunrise during early summer and early winter) and the Lord of the two wests (places of sunset during early summer and early winter).

55:18 18. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?
