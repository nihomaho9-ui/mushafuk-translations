38:62 62. And they will say: "What is the matter with us that we see not men whom we used to count among the bad ones?"

38:63 63. Did we take them as an object of mockery, or have (our) eyes failed to perceive them?"

38:64 64. Verily, that is the very truth - the mutual dispute of the people of the Fire!

38:66 66. "The Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, the All-Mighty, the Oft-Forgiving."

38:67 67. Say: "That (this Qur’ân) is a great news,

38:68 68. "From which you turn away!

38:69 69. "I had no knowledge of the chiefs (angels) on high when they were disputing and discussing (about the creation of Adam).

38:70 70. "Only this has been revealed to me, that I am a plain warner."

38:71 71. (Remember) when your Lord said to the angels: "Truly I am going to create man from clay".

38:72 72. So when I have fashioned him and breathed into him (his) soul created by Me, then you fall down prostrate to him."

38:73 73. So the angels prostrated themselves, all of them:

38:74 74. Except Iblîs (Satan): he was proud[2] and was one of the disbelievers.

38:75 75. (Allâh) said: "O Iblîs (Satan)! What prevents you from prostrating yourself to one whom I have created with Both My Hands[3]. Are you too proud (to fall prostrate to Adam) or are you one of the high exalted?"

38:76 76. [Iblîs (Satan)] said: "I am better than he. You created me from fire, and You created him from clay."

38:77 77. (Allâh) said: "Then get out from here; for verily you are outcast.

38:78 78. "And verily My Curse is on you till the Day of Recompense."
