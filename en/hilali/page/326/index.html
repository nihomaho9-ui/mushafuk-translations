Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 326

21:45 45. Say (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم): "I warn you only by the revelation (from Allâh and not by the opinion of the religious scholars and others). But the deaf (who follow the religious scholars and others blindly) will not hear the call, (even) when they are warned [i.e. one should follow only the Qur’ân and the Sunnah (legal ways, orders, acts of worship, and the statements of Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, as the companions of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم did)][3].

21:46 46. And if a breath (minor calamity) of the Torment of your Lord touches them, they will surely cry: "Woe unto us! Indeed we have been Zâlimûn (polytheists and wrong-doers)."

21:47 47. And We shall set up balances of justice on the Day of Resurrection, then none will be dealt with unjustly in anything. And if there be the weight of a mustard seed, We will bring it. And Sufficient are We to take account.

21:48 48. And indeed We granted to Mûsâ (Moses) and Hârûn (Aaron) the criterion (of right and wrong), and a shining light [i.e. the Taurât (Torah)] and a Reminder for Al-Muttaqûn (the pious - See V.2:2).

21:49 49. Those who fear their Lord without seeing Him, and they are afraid of the Hour.

21:50 50. And this is a blessed Reminder (the Qur’ân) which We have sent down: will you then (dare to) deny it?[4]

21:51 51. And indeed We bestowed aforetime on Ibrâhîm (Abraham) his (portion of) guidance, and We were Well-Acquainted with him (as to his Belief in the Oneness of Allâh).

21:52 52. When he said to his father and his people: "What are these images, to which you are devoted?"

21:53 53. They said: "We found our fathers worshipping them."

21:54 54. He said: "Indeed, you and your fathers have been in manifest error."

21:55 55. They said: "Have you brought us the truth, or are you one of those who play about?"

21:56 56. He said: "Nay, your Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the earth, Who created them and to that I am one of the witnesses.

21:57 57. "And by Allâh, I shall plot a plan (to destroy) your idols after you have gone away and turned your backs."
