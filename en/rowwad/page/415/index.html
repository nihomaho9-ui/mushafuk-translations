Rowwad Translation Center — 415

32:1 Alif Lām Mīm[1].

32:2 The revelation of the Book, about which there is no doubt, is from the Lord of the worlds.

32:3 Or do they say, “He has fabricated it.”? No, it is the truth from your Lord so that you may warn a people to whom no warner has come before you, in order that they may be guided.

32:4 It is Allah Who created the heavens and earth and all that is between them in six Days, then rose over the Throne[1]. You have no protector or intercessor other than Him. Will you not then take heed?

32:5 He manages every matter from the heaven to the earth, then everything ascends to Him on a Day the length of which is a thousand years by your reckoning.

32:6 Such is the All-Knower of the unseen and the seen, the All-Mighty, the Most Merciful,

32:7 Who perfected everything He created, and initiated the creation of man[1] from clay.

32:8 Then He made his progeny from the extract of a worthless fluid.

32:9 Then He fashioned him and breathed into him of His spirit[1]. He granted you hearing, sight and intellect; yet little it is that you give thanks.

32:10 They say, “What! Is it that when we are disintegrated into the earth, will we then be created anew?” Rather, they deny the meeting with their Lord.

32:11 Say, “The angel of death, who is in charge of you, will take your souls, then to your Lord you will be brought back.”
