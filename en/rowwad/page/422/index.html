Rowwad Translation Center — 422

33:31 But whoever among you devoutly obeys Allah and His Messenger and does righteous deeds, We will give her a double reward, and We have prepared for her an honorable provision.

33:32 O wives of the Prophet, you are not like any other women: if you fear Allah, do not be complaisant in your speech[1], lest those with sickness in their hearts should be moved with desire; but speak in an appropriate manner.

33:33 Stay in your homes and do not display your charms as was done in the former days of ignorance; establish prayer and give Zakah; and obey Allah and His Messenger. Allah only intends to keep evil away from you, O members of the [Prophet’s] household, and to completely purify you.

33:34 And remember what is recited in your homes of Allah’s verses and [prophetic] wisdom[1]. Allah is Most Subtle, All-Aware.

33:35 Muslim men and women, believing men and women, obedient men and women, truthful men and women, patient men and women, humble men and women, charitable men and women, fasting men and women, and the men and women who guard their chastity, and men and women who remember Allah much – Allah has prepared for them forgiveness and a great reward.
