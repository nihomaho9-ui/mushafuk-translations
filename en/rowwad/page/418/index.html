Rowwad Translation Center — 418

33:1 O Prophet, fear Allah and do not obey the disbelievers and the hypocrites. Indeed, Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise.

33:2 Follow what is revealed to you from your Lord. Indeed, Allah is All-Aware of what you do.

33:3 Put your trust in Allah; sufficient is Allah as a Disposer of Affairs.

33:4 Allah has not made for any man two hearts inside him, nor has he made your wives – whom you declare unlawful – your real mothers[1], nor has he made your adopted sons your real sons[2]. These are only words that you utter, but Allah speaks the truth and He guides to the right way.

33:5 Call these [adopted sons] by their fathers’ names; that is more equitable before Allah. But if you do not know their fathers, then they are your brothers in faith and your patronized associates. There is no blame on you if you make a mistake in that regard, but only for what your hearts intend. And Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.

33:6 The Prophet has a greater claim over the believers than their own selves[1], and his wives are their mothers. And blood relatives are more entitled [to inheritance] than other believers and emigrants according to the Book of Allah unless you do good to your close associates [through bequest][2]. This is written in the Record[3].
