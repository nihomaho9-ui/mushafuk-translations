Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 604

112:1 1. Say (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم): "He is Allâh, (the) One.[1]

112:2 2. "Allâh-us-Samad (السيد الذي يصمد إليه في الحاجات) [Allâh the Self-Sufficient Master, Whom all creatures need, (He neither eats nor drinks)].

112:3 3. "He begets not, nor was He begotten.[2]

112:4 4. "And there is none co-equal or comparable unto Him."

113:1 1. Say: "I seek refuge with (Allâh), the Lord of the daybreak,

113:2 2. "From the evil of what He has created,

113:3 3. "And from the evil of the darkening (night) as it comes with its darkness; (or the moon as it sets or goes away),

113:4 4. "And from the evil of those who practise witchcraft when they blow in the knots,

113:5 5. "And from the evil of the envier when he envies."

114:1 1. Say: "I seek refuge with (Allâh) the Lord of mankind,[1]

114:2 2. "The King of mankind -[2]

114:3 3. "The Ilâh (God) of mankind,

114:4 4. "From the evil of the whisperer (devil who whispers evil in the hearts of men) who withdraws (from his whispering in one’s heart after one remembers Allâh).[3]

114:5 5. "Who whispers in the breasts of mankind.

114:6 6. "Of jinn and men."
