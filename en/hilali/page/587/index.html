Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 587

82:1 1. When the heaven is cleft asunder.

82:2 2. And when the stars have fallen and scattered.

82:3 3. And when the seas are burst forth.

82:4 4. And when the graves are turned upside down (and bring out their contents)

82:5 5. (Then) a person will know what he has sent forward and (what he has) left behind (of good or bad deeds).

82:6 6. O man! What has made you careless about your Lord, the Most Generous?

82:7 7. Who created you, fashioned you perfectly, and gave you due proportion.

82:8 8. In whatever form He willed, He put you together.

82:9 9. Nay! But you deny Ad-Dîn (i.e. the Day of Recompense).

82:10 10. But verily, over you (are appointed angels in charge of mankind) to watch you,[1]

82:11 11. Kirâman (Honourable) Kâtibîn writing down (your deeds),[2]

82:12 12. They know all that you do.

82:13 13. Verily, the Abrâr (the pious believers of Islamic Monotheism) will be in Delight (Paradise);

82:14 14. And verily, the Fujjâr (the wicked, disbelievers, polytheists, sinners and evil-doers) will be in the blazing Fire (Hell),

82:15 15. Therein they will enter, and taste its burning flame on the Day of Recompense,

82:16 16. And they (Al-Fujjâr) will not be absent therefrom.

82:17 17. And what will make you know what the Day of Recompense is?

82:18 18. Again, what will make you know what the Day of Recompense is?

82:19 19. (It will be) the Day when no person shall have power (to do) anything for another, and the Decision, that Day, will be (wholly) with Allâh.

83:1 1. Woe to Al-Mutaffifûn (those who give less in measure and weight).

83:2 2. Those who, when they have to receive by measure from men, demand full measure,

83:3 3. And when they have to give by measure or weight to (other) men, give less than due.
