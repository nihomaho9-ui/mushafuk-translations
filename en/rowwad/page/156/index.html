Rowwad Translation Center — 156

7:44 The people of Paradise will call out to the people of Hell, “We have surely found our Lord’s promise to be true. Have you also found your Lord’s promise to be true[1]?” They will say, “Yes.” Then a caller will announce among them, “May Allah’s curse be upon the wrongdoers,

7:45 those who prevent [people] from the way of Allah, seeking to make it crooked, and who disbelieve in the Hereafter.”

7:46 Between them there will be a barrier[1], and on its Heights there will be men[2] who will recognize each group by their marks. They will call out to the people of Paradise, “Peace be on you.” They will not have entered it, yet they will eagerly hope [to enter].

7:47 And when their eyes are turned towards the people of Hell, they will say, “Our Lord, do not join us with the wrongdoing people.”

7:48 The people of the Heights will call out to some men whom they recognize by their marks, saying, “Neither your great numbers[1] nor your arrogance were of any avail to you.”

7:49 “Are these the ones whom you swore that Allah would never grant mercy?” “Enter Paradise; you will have no fear, nor will you grieve.”

7:50 The people of the Fire will call out to the people of Paradise, “Send down to us some water or any provision that Allah has given you.” They will say, “Allah has forbidden both to the disbelievers,

7:51 those who took their religion as amusement and play[1], and were deceived by the life of this world. Today We will ignore them just as they ignored their meeting of this Day and because they used to reject Our verses.”
