Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 589

83:34 34. But this Day (the Day of Resurrection) those who believe will laugh at the disbelievers[2]

83:35 35. On (high) thrones, looking (at all things).

83:36 36. Are not the disbelievers paid (fully) for what they used to do?

84:1 1. When the heaven is split asunder,

84:2 2. And listens to and obeys its Lord - and it must do so.

84:3 3. And when the earth is stretched forth,

84:4 4. And has cast out all that was in it and became empty.

84:5 5. And listens to and obeys its Lord - and it must do so.

84:6 6. O man! Verily, you are returning towards your Lord with your deeds and actions (good or bad), a sure returning, and you will meet (the results of your deeds which you did).

84:7 7. Then as for him who will be given his Record in his right hand,

84:8 8. He surely will receive an easy reckoning,

84:9 9. And will return to his family in joy!

84:10 10. But whosoever is given his Record behind his back,

84:11 11. He will invoke (for his) destruction,

84:12 12. And he shall enter a blazing Fire, and be made to taste its burning.

84:13 13. Verily, he was among his people in joy!

84:14 14. Verily, he thought that he would never come back (to Us)!

84:15 15. Yes! Verily, his Lord has been ever beholding him!

84:16 16. So I swear by the afterglow of sunset;

84:17 17. And by the night and whatever it gathers in its darkness,

84:18 18. And by the moon when it is at the full.

84:19 19. You shall certainly travel from stage to stage (in this life and in the Hereafter).

84:20 20. What is the matter with them, that they believe not?

84:21 21. And when the Qur’ân is recited to them, they fall not prostrate.

84:22 22. Nay, those who disbelieve belie (Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم and whatever he brought, i.e. this Qur’ân and Islâmic Monotheism, etc.).[1]

84:23 23. And Allâh knows best what they gather (of good and bad deeds),

84:24 24. So announce to them a painful torment.
