Rowwad Translation Center — 533

55:42 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:43 [They will be told] “This is the Hell that the wicked used to deny.”

55:44 They will go round between it and scalding water.

55:45 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny[1]?

55:46 For those who fear of standing before their Lord[1] will be two Gardens.

55:47 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:48 Shaded by spreading branches.

55:49 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:50 In each there are two flowing springs.

55:51 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:52 In each there are two kinds of every fruit.

55:53 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:54 They will recline on couches lined with rich brocade, with the fruits of both gardens within reach.

55:55 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:56 In them there will be maidens of restrained gaze[1], untouched[2] before them by any man or jinn.

55:57 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:58 As if they were rubies and pearls[1].

55:59 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:60 Is the reward for goodness anything but goodness?

55:61 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:62 And besides these two there will be two other gardens.

55:63 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:64 Both of the deepest green.

55:65 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:66 In each there are two gushing springs.

55:67 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?

55:68 In each there are fruits, palm trees, and pomegranates.

55:69 Then which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?
