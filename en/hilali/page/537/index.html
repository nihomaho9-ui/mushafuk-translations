Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 537

56:77 77. That (this) is indeed an honourable recitation (the Noble Qur’ân).

56:78 78. In a Book well-guarded (with Allâh in the heaven i.e. Al-Lauh Al-Mahfûz ).

56:79 79. Which (that Book with Allâh) none can touch but the purified (i.e. the angels).

56:80 80. A Revelation (this Qur’ân) from the Lord of the ‘Âlamîn (mankind, jinn and all that exists).

56:81 81. Is it such a talk (this Qur’ân) that you (disbelievers) deny?

56:82 82. And instead (of thanking Allâh) for the provision He gives you, you deny (Him by disbelief)!

56:83 83. Then why do you not (intervene) when (the soul of a dying person) reaches the throat?

56:84 84. And you at the moment are looking on,

56:85 85. But We (i.e. Our angels who take the soul) are nearer to him than you, but you see not, (Tafsir At-Tabarî)

56:86 86. Then why do you not - if you are exempt from the reckoning and recompense (punishment) -

56:87 87. Bring back the soul (to its body), if you are truthful?

56:88 88. Then, if he (the dying person) be of the Muqarrabûn (those brought near to Allâh),

56:89 89. (There is for him) rest and provision, and a Garden of Delights (Paradise).

56:90 90. And if he (the dying person) be of those on the Right Hand,

56:91 91. Then there is safety and peace (from the Punishment of Allâh) for those on the right Hand.

56:92 92. But if he (the dying person) be of the denying (of the Resurrection), the erring (away from the Right Path of Islâmic Monotheism),

56:93 93. Then for him is an entertainment with boiling water.

56:94 94. And burning in Hell-fire.

56:95 95. Verily, this! This is an absolute Truth with certainty.

56:96 96. So glorify with praises the Name of your Lord, the Most Great.

57:1 1. Whatsoever is in the heavens and the earth glorifies Allâh - and He is the All-Mighty, All-Wise.

57:2 2. His is the kingdom of the heavens and the earth. It is He Who gives life and causes death: and He is Able to do all things.

57:3 3. He is the First (nothing is before Him) and the Last (nothing is after Him), the Most High (nothing is above Him) and the Most Near (nothing is nearer than Him). And He is the All-Knower of every thing.
