Rowwad Translation Center — 67

3:133 And hasten towards forgiveness from your Lord and a Paradise as wide as the heavens and earth, prepared for the righteous,

3:134 those who spend in times of prosperity and adversity, and who restrain their anger[1] and pardon people; for Allah loves those who do good.

3:135 And those who, when they commit a shameful act or wrong themselves, remember Allah and seek forgiveness for their sins[1] – who can forgive sins except Allah? – and they do not persist in what they did knowingly.

3:136 Their reward is forgiveness from their Lord and gardens under which rivers flow, abiding therein forever. How excellent is the reward of those who do [righteous] deeds!

3:137 There were nations that passed before you; so travel through the land and see how was the end of the deniers[1].

3:138 This is an exhortation for mankind; a guidance and admonition for the righteous.

3:139 Do not lose heart nor grieve, for you have the upper hand, if you are [truly] believers.

3:140 If you have suffered a blow [at ’Uhud], they too have suffered a blow like it [at Badr]. We alternate among people these days, so that Allah may make evident the believers and may choose martyrs from among you. And Allah does not like the wrongdoers.
